🌱 Project Purpose — About the Smart Crop Prediction System

Choosing the right crop for agricultural land is one of the most critical decisions in farming. Incorrect crop selection leads to reduced yields, soil nutrient depletion, and heavy economic loss.

The Smart Crop Prediction System helps farmers and students identify the optimal crop for any land parcel by evaluating key soil chemical nutrients (Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium), soil pH acidity, and ambient weather parameters (Temperature, Humidity, Rainfall).

🧠 Machine Learning Algorithms

Trained and compared on 2,200 real agricultural records across 22 crop classes

Four candidate machine learning algorithms were trained with stratified 80/20 train/test splits and standardized input scaling:

🌲 Random Forest (Selected Best Model)

Ensemble of 100 decision trees combining bagging and feature sub-sampling to prevent overfitting.

Accuracy: 99.55% • F1: 0.9955
🌿 Decision Tree

Hierarchical rule-based tree splitting decisions based on Gini impurity.

Accuracy: 97.95% • F1: 0.9794
📍 K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN)

Distance-based classification grouping points in 7-dimensional normalized feature space.

Accuracy: 97.95% • F1: 0.9793
📈 Logistic Regression

Multinomial linear model establishing decision boundaries across standardized features.

Accuracy: 97.27% • F1: 0.9725
